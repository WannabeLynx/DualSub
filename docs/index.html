Dual Subtitles — Two Languages at Once

A free Chrome extension that shows two subtitle languages at the same time while you watch Netflix. Perfect for language learning and bilingual viewing.

Add to Chrome View on GitHub

What it does

  • Shows two subtitles on screen at once — dual / double / bilingual subtitles
  • Pick any two languages the title offers
  • Choose which language is on top, with adjustable size and position
  • Uses the official subtitle tracks — no machine translation
  • No account, no tracking, nothing leaves your browser

Who it's for

People learning a language who want to read it next to their own, bilingual households, and anyone who likes comparing translations.
