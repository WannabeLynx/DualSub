# Privacy Policy — Dual Subtitles

_Last updated: 2026-06-24_

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- It runs only on Netflix watch pages.
- Your language and display preferences are stored locally in your browser
  (`chrome.storage`) and are never sent anywhere.
- It downloads subtitle files from the streaming service's own content servers solely to
  display them on the page you are watching. These files are not stored or shared.
- There is no analytics, tracking, advertising, fingerprinting, or account system.
- No data is sold or shared with third parties.
