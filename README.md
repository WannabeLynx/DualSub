# Dual Subtitles — Two Subtitle Languages at Once

A free Chrome extension that shows **two subtitles at the same time** while you watch
**Netflix** — for example your native language together with the language you're learning.
Also called dual subtitles, double subtitles, or bilingual subtitles.

> Watch with two languages on screen at once. Great for **language learning** and
> bilingual households.

## Features

- 🎬 **Two subtitles at once** — show two languages stacked on screen, perfectly in sync
- 🌍 **Any pair the title offers** — uses the official subtitle tracks, no machine translation
- 🔝 Choose which language sits on top
- 🔠 Adjustable font size and position
- 🪟 Optional background behind the text for readability
- 💾 Remembers your choice per title
- 🔒 No account, no tracking, nothing leaves your browser — it's free

## Install

**From the Chrome Web Store:** _(link coming once published)_

**Manually (developer mode):**
1. Download or clone this repo.
2. Go to `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select the folder containing `manifest.json`.
4. Open a video, press play, click the toolbar icon, and pick your two languages.

> Requires Chrome 111 or newer.

## How to use

1. Open a title and press play.
2. Click the **Dual Subtitles** icon in your toolbar.
3. Pick a **primary** and a **secondary** language — both appear on screen right away.

## Who it's for

- People **learning a language** who want to read it next to their own
- **Bilingual households** watching together
- Anyone who wants to **compare translations**

## How it works

The extension reads the subtitle tracks the streaming service already provides for the
title you're watching, fetches the two you pick, and draws them in a small overlay that
stays in sync with the video. Everything happens locally in your browser.

## Privacy

No data collection, no analytics, no account. Your settings are stored locally
(`chrome.storage`). See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
